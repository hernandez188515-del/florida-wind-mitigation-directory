A complete list of current, active Florida-licensed home inspectors whose official mailing county is Pinellas, plus a shorter list of companies checked on November 25, 2026 (America/New_York) because their own websites say they offer wind mitigation or 4-point inspections.

DBPR extract downloaded Nov 19, 2026 329 inspectors in the main table All licenses shown expire 07/31/2028 in this file

Read this first. A Florida home-inspector license is necessary but not always sufficient to sign a wind mitigation form. Under Florida law, a licensed home inspector who signs the Uniform Mitigation Verification Inspection Form (OIR-B1-1802) must also have completed the required hurricane-mitigation training and proficiency exam (see the Florida DFS summary of who may sign). A home-inspector license alone is not proof of that eligibility, and this directory cannot see that training in the public license extract. Confirm the inspector can complete the current OIR-B1-1802 form, and re-check the license on the official DBPR search before you book. Listings are not endorsements, and we do not collect a referral fee from anyone listed here today.
