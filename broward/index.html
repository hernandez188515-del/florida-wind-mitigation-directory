Read this first. A Florida home-inspector license is necessary but not always sufficient to sign a wind mitigation form. Under Florida law, a licensed home inspector who signs the Uniform Mitigation Verification Inspection Form (OIR-B1-1802) must also have completed the required hurricane-mitigation training and proficiency exam (see the Florida DFS summary of who may sign). A home-inspector license alone is not proof of that eligibility, and this directory cannot see that training in the public license extract. Confirm the inspector can complete the current OIR-B1-1802 form, and re-check the license on the official DBPR search before you book.
