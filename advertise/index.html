Are you a licensed inspector? Get a featured listing.

Each county page on this site lists every current, active Florida home-inspector license in that county from the official DBPR file. A featured listing puts your company, the insurance inspections you offer, and your published prices at the top of the page for the counties you serve.

What a featured listing includes

Top placement

Your company appears first in the “Companies that advertise these inspections” section of each county page you choose, above the non-featured companies, with a clear “Featured” label.

Your details, checked

Company name, phone, website link, service area by city, and the inspections you offer — wind mitigation (OIR-B1-1802 Rev. 04/26), 4-point, roof certification, full home inspection — plus prices only if they are published on your own site.

Links to your booking page

A direct link to your scheduling or contact page, so homeowners with a renewal deadline can book without hunting for it.

Who qualifies

  • Your license must appear as current and active in the Florida DBPR license file (home inspector, contractor, engineer, architect, or building code inspector).
  • Your own website must say you offer wind mitigation, 4-point, or roof inspections.
  • We confirm wind-mitigation signing eligibility with you, because the public license file does not show the hurricane-mitigation training required under s. 627.711, Fla. Stat.

What stays the same for homeowners

  • The full license table on every county page still lists every current, active licensee, in the same order, whether or not they pay.
  • Paid placement is always labeled “Featured.” We do not write reviews, star ratings, or “best of” claims for featured companies.
  • We never publish home street addresses from the license file.
